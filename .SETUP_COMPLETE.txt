================================================================================
                        TROUBLESHOOTING
================================================================================

Q: "GROQ_API_KEY not found"
A: cp .env.example .env && nano .env
   Add your key from https://console.groq.com

Q: "Metasploit not found"
A: apt install metasploit-framework

Q: "No findings discovered"
A: Check if target is online: ping 10.10.10.40
   Try without ping: nmap -Pn 10.10.10.40

Q: "Shell callback not received"
A: Check firewall: sudo ufw allow 4444/tcp
